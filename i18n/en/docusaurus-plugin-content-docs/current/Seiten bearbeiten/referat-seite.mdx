Departments (*Referate*) and autonomous groups have their own pages under **AStA
Referate** (English: **AStA Societies**, URL path `/en/asta-departments/...`). Alongside
the page body, you maintain four settings directly in the right editor sidebar (**Page**
tab)—inserting a separate card pattern into the page content is no longer needed.

:::info Which fields appear where
- **Featured image**, **Order**, and **Badge (Startseite)** control your department's
  tile in the [Referate Grid](./bausteine.mdx#referate-grid) on the homepage.
- **Übersichtskarte (Referat)** generates the info box (*Contact & meetings* on English
  pages / *Kontakt & Treffen* on German pages) directly on your department page.
:::

## 1. Featured image (logo) and Order

1. Open the department page and select the **Page** tab in the right sidebar.
2. Under **Set featured image**, choose the department's logo from the Media Library
   (preferably SVG or PNG with a transparent background) and enter a short
   **Alternative Text** (for example, the name of the department).
3. Under **Page Attributes → Order**, enter a number if needed. It determines the tile's
   position in the **Referate Grid**; without a number, the overview sorts alphabetically
   by page title.

Further tips on replacing a logo are in [Gallery](../galerie.mdx).

## 2. Badge (Startseite)

The **Badge (Startseite)** panel appears in the right sidebar **only on German department
pages** (direct child pages of *AStA Referate*). Even when your WordPress user profile is
set to English, this panel and all of its labels appear in German. It sets the small
pill badge in the bottom-right corner of your homepage tile.

Six modes are available under *Art des Badges*:

| Mode | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Automatisch** | Derives the badge automatically from the saved meeting time (*Treffzeit*). |
| **Regelmäßig** | Structured inputs for *Wochentag* (weekday, *Montag*–*Sonntag*), *Zeit* (start time), *bis* (optional end time), and *Raum / Ort* (room/location; note: *"Campus Lüneburg weglassen. Rotes Feld nur wenn nötig."*). |
| **Nach Ankündigung** | Shows the fixed label `nach Ankündigung`. |
| **Online** | Shows the fixed label `Online`. |
| **Eigener Text** | Free-text field with at most **32 characters** (`… / 32 Zeichen`) and clickable *Beispiele* chips (e.g. `Mi 18:00 · C14.102`, `Do 16:00 · WoZi`, `Online`, `nach Ankündigung`). |
| **Kein Badge** | Hides the badge on the homepage tile completely. |

Directly below the options, the live preview (*Vorschau*) renders the finished tile with
its featured image (or the page title as fallback) and the current badge text in real
time.

:::caution Keep it short
Badges sit on top of the logo on the tile. In **Regelmäßig** or **Eigener Text** mode,
use only weekday, time, and a short room code (maximum 32 characters) instead of full
sentences.
:::

## 3. Übersichtskarte (Referat)

The **Übersichtskarte (Referat)** panel appears on **both German and English department
pages** and controls the info box (*Contact & meetings* on `/en/asta-departments/...` /
*Kontakt & Treffen* on German pages) on that department page. Its field labels in the
sidebar remain in German even under an English WordPress profile, while the live card
preview (*Vorschau*) and the published card on `/en/asta-departments/...` render in
English.

The panel provides nine fields:

| Field | Note |
|---|---|
| **Rolle/Label** | Optional heading for the contact row (default: "Contact person" / "Ansprechpartner*in") |
| **Ansprechpartner*in** | Name(s) of the responsible contact person(s) |
| **Zeiten-Label** | Optional heading for the meeting time (default: "Meeting" / "Treffen") |
| **Treffzeit** | When the department meets or holds office hours |
| **Raum-Label** | Optional heading for the location (default: "Room" / "Raum") |
| **Raum/Ort** | Building and room number or meeting point |
| **E-Mail** | Department email address (rendered as a `mailto:` link) |
| **Instagram** | Handle only without URL, e.g. `asta_lueneburg` (without `@`) |
| **Telegram** | Full URL, e.g. `https://t.me/…` |

:::caution Minimum rule: 2 content fields
**Fill in at least 2 content fields for the card to appear on the page.** If fewer than
two content fields are filled in, the website hides the overview card automatically and
the live preview (*Vorschau*) in the panel displays a notice.
:::

## 4. Do not insert a Referat Card pattern in the content

The former *Referat Card* pattern was removed from the block inserter. Maintain
department contact details, meeting times, and social media links exclusively through
**Übersichtskarte (Referat)** and **Badge (Startseite)**—for general service pages, the
**AStA Contact Card** pattern remains available under
[Building Blocks (Templates)](./bausteine.mdx).
