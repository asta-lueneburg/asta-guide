# Creating Posts

Do you have news from your department, service unit, or the StuPa? Anyone with an
account can publish **Posts**. They appear under "News" (*Aktuelles*), on the homepage,
and in category listings.

:::info Post or page?
A post is a blog article: dated, categorized, shown in chronological listings, and moves
downward over time. A page is the permanent place for long-term information and lives in
the menu. When in doubt: use a post for news, and a page for anything that should still
be accurate six months from now.
:::

## Four components

A post consists of a **Title**, **Excerpt**, **Featured image**, and **Content**.

### 1. Title

Keep it specific and free of clickbait. The title is the heading of the post and appears
in listings and in the browser tab.

If you change the title later, the URL (slug) does not update automatically.

### 2. Excerpt

The excerpt is **not optional polish**: the theme uses it as the `meta description` for
search engines and as the description text in social media previews. You will find it in
the right sidebar under the **Post** tab under **Excerpt**; it starts collapsed as **Add an
excerpt…**.

- Two to three sentences, **120 to 155 characters**
- Reads like natural prose, not a bulleted keyword list
- Summarizes what the post is about—avoid "click here to read more"

<ImageAside src="/img/guide-gwyneth-en/editor-seitenleiste.png" third alt="Right sidebar in the Post tab showing Set featured image, Status, Excerpt, and Categories">
At the top you will see <strong>Set featured image</strong>, and further down the link{' '}
<strong>Add an excerpt…</strong>. Clicking it opens the excerpt text box.
</ImageAside>

:::caution Important
If the Excerpt is left empty, the theme falls back to the **first paragraph** of the
post. If there is no text paragraph either, search engines only show a generic fallback
with the title. Writing a dedicated excerpt is always better.
:::

### 3. Featured image

The image shown on cards, listings, and link previews. In the right sidebar under the
**Post** tab, click **Set featured image** and choose an image from the Media Library or
upload a new one.

- Without a featured image, the image area on the card remains empty.
- Add **Alternative Text** when uploading (see
  [Image Descriptions (Alt Text)](./barrierefreiheit/bilder-alt-texte)).
- For social media previews, the featured image serves as the preview image.

<Screenshot

### 4. Content

The main body text, built from blocks. The same rules apply as on pages: start content
headings at **H2**, and do not set custom font sizes or colors manually. See
[Page Structure & Layout](./Seiten%20bearbeiten/seiten-aufbau) for details.

## Category

Every post belongs in **one** category. The category determines where the post appears
(German posts use the German categories shown below; English posts use their English
counterparts):

| Category (DE / EN) | Appears under |
|---|---|
| **Aktuelles → AStA News** / **News → AStA News** | `/news/aktuelles/asta-news/` (`/en/news/news-en/asta-news-en/`) |
| **Aktuelles → Veranstaltungen** / **News → Events** | `/news/aktuelles/veranstaltungen/` (`/en/news/news-en/events/`) |
| **Aktuelles → Newsletter** | `/news/aktuelles/newsletter/` |
| **Aktuelles → Jobs (extern)** | `/news/aktuelles/jobs-extern/` |
| **Aktuelles → Sonstiges** | `/news/aktuelles/sonstiges/` |
| **Jobs (AStA)** | `/news/jobs/` |
| **StuPa** | `/news/stupa/` |
| **Referate** / **Departments** | `/news/referate/` |
| **Wohnen** / **Housing** | `/news/wohnen/` |

Select the category in the right sidebar under the **Post** tab in **Categories**.

<ImageAside src="/img/guide-gwyneth-en/kategorien.png" third alt="Categories panel in the right sidebar showing the category hierarchy">
Categories are shown as a hierarchy in the sidebar: parent category <strong>Aktuelles</strong>{' '}
with its subcategories underneath. Check one row, not several.
</ImageAside>

:::caution Important
- **One** category is enough. Selecting multiple categories creates duplicate entries in
  listings.
- **Never select "Uncategorized" (*Unkategorisiert*).** This category must remain empty.
- If your category is missing, do not pick an unrelated one—ask the webmaster.
:::

## No sidebars anymore

The old "Sidebars" tab and the option to assign an organization sidebar were **removed**
with the relaunch. Posts are displayed full-width; state who the post is from in the text
or via the post's **Author** field.

## Publishing

Click **Publish** in the top right. The **Preview** button lets you check the post before
anyone else sees it.

You can also prepare a post and schedule it for later: under **Publish** (next to
*Status*), click **Immediately** and set the desired date and time. The post will go live
automatically at that time.

:::caution Important
Always check the preview before publishing. Once a post is published, every visitor to the
website can see it.
:::

## Two rules that are often forgotten

- **Never publish "text coming soon."** If a section is missing, leave it out or save the
  post as a **Draft**. Placeholders are removed by the editorial team.
- **Link instead of duplicating.** If information is already on a service page, link to it
  rather than copying it. Otherwise two separate versions will drift out of sync.
