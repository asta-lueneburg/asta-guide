# Bed Exchange

import Screenshot from '@site/src/components/Screenshot';

The Bed Exchange (*Bettenbörse*) arranges temporary places to sleep: connecting students
who have a spare sofa or bed with first-year students looking for a place for their
first few weeks.

Publicly, it is located on the
[Bed exchange](https://asta-lueneburg.de/en/service/bed-exchange/) page (German:
[Bettenbörse](https://asta-lueneburg.de/service/bettenboerse/)).

> **Changed with the relaunch:** There is no dedicated "Bettenbörse" plugin anymore. The
> page is a regular WordPress page containing a **Fluent Forms** form; the old plugin
> setup and `ASTA_ENV` instructions no longer apply.

## How submissions arrive

The form embedded on the page is the Fluent Forms form **`Bettenboerse (ID 1)`**. Every
submission appears as an entry in the backend:

1. Open **Fluent Forms → Entries**.
2. Select the **`Bettenboerse (ID 1)`** form.
3. View, filter, and open individual entries.

Entries contain personal contact details. Only process them inside the backend—do not
export them into group chats or private spreadsheets.

## Matching

The Bed Exchange puts people in touch; it does not match them automatically:

1. Pair compatible entries (location, dates, number of beds).
2. Introduce both sides to each other via email.
3. Note the status on the entry after matching.

## Opening and closing

The campaign runs at the start of the winter semester. A dedicated paragraph on the page
states the current status—for example:

> **The Bed Exchange is closed for Winter Semester 25/26.**
> *(German: „Die Bettenbörse ist für das Wintersemester 25/26 geschlossen.“)*

To open the exchange, this notice is replaced or removed; to close it, it is put back.
The webmaster makes this edit upon request from the responsible department.

## What does not belong on the page

- **Long-term rental listings.** Use the
  [notice board / post submission form](https://asta-lueneburg.de/service/beitrag-einreichen/)
  for those instead.
- **Contact details as plain text.** They belong in the form, not in the page copy.
- **Confirmed bed promises.** The page describes the service; it does not allocate beds
  itself.

:::caution Data protection
Entries contain names, email addresses, and location details. They must not be
forwarded, shared in group chats, or exported to cloud spreadsheets. Once the campaign
ends, the entries are deleted; deletion is performed by the webmaster.
:::
