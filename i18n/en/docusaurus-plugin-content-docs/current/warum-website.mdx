# Website — Why and Who?

## Why does the website exist?

On the website, all kinds of information are publicly available and easy to look up. This
includes current news—which can be embedded as blog posts or Instagram feeds—as well as
important information about service units (*Servicebetriebe*) and departments
(*Referate*), the work of the StuPa, and much more.

The website aims to bring together all relevant information about the AStA's work for
students, AStA members, prospective students, and the wider public. This makes the website
a central point of contact for many different people looking for specific information or
simply browsing.

## Who is responsible?

The AStA employs a webmaster who is responsible for keeping the AStA website running. The
focus of this role is not on content, but on functionality. This includes regular updates,
documentation, and coordinating with website users, AStA departments, service units, the
StuPa, the office team, the Speakers (*Sprecher\*innen*), and anyone else who wants to
publish content on the site, ensuring everything works as intended.

The webmaster is available for any questions about the website! Feel free to reach out at
webmaster@asta-lueneburg.de.

## Who creates the content?

You are responsible for the content—the AStA departments, service units, project leads, the
StuPa, and everyone else who has something to publish on the website. If you belong to
one of these groups, this guide is for you.

### How can I edit website content?

That is exactly what the website guide you are reading right now explains. The sidebar on
the left gives you an overview of the different options—feel free to click through!

:::info Disclaimer
Not all areas or functions of the website are accessible to everyone. Above all, this
keeps things clear and ensures that nobody accidentally changes website settings that
should not be changed.
:::

To edit content, each of you has a user account to log in to the website and make
changes. You can find more details in the "Accounts & Passwords" chapter in the left
sidebar.

## Responsibilities at a glance

| Level | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Content** | The respective department or service unit for its own pages and posts |
| **Technical** | The webmaster role: theme, updates, user accounts, menu and plugin settings |
| **Legal** | The AStA Executive Board (*Vorstand*). The imprint (*Impressum*) and privacy policy are never changed without consultation |

Editing permissions are assigned per department or service unit, not globally. If you
cannot see a page that you should be able to edit, contact the webmaster instead of
creating a second account.

## What the website is not

- **Not a replacement for Instagram or Telegram.** The website is the archive and the
  reference; social media is the fast channel. A post on the website stays searchable and
  findable, while a feed post disappears.
- **Not a file storage drive.** Images and PDFs belong in the Media Library, not as loose
  attachments scattered across pages.
- **Not a place for unfinished drafts.** Anything published is visible to everyone—keep
  drafts saved as drafts.
