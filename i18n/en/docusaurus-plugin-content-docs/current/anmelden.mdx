# Accounts & Passwords

## Logging in to the website

You can find the AStA website at [https://asta-lueneburg.de](https://asta-lueneburg.de).

To sign in, go to [https://asta-lueneburg.de/login](https://asta-lueneburg.de/login).
This address opens the WordPress login screen (`asta-lueneburg.de/wp-login.php` is the
same page). Enter your credentials and click **Log In**.

After signing in, you land on the **Dashboard**. From there, two main areas take you
further:

| Area | Purpose | Appears in |
|---|---|---|
| **Posts** | News, reports, job postings, events | News listings and on the homepage |
| **Pages** | Permanent content such as Services, Departments, StuPa | The navigation menu |

The **admin bar** also appears at the top of the public site while you are signed in. From
any public page, it takes you straight into the editor for the content you are currently
viewing.

:::info
If you do not have login credentials (yet) or have forgotten them, contact the webmaster
at webmaster@asta-lueneburg.de. Do not create a second account—permissions are tied to
your account.
:::

## Changing your password

Everyone is encouraged to change their password after logging in for the first time and
at regular intervals afterwards.

1. Click your name in the top right → **Edit Profile**
   (or in the admin sidebar: **Users → Profile**).
2. Scroll down to **Account Management** and click **Generate Password** (under
   *New Password*). WordPress suggests a strong password; you can overwrite the field if
   needed.
3. Click **Update Profile**. Your previous password immediately becomes invalid.

<Screenshot
  src="/img/guide-gwyneth-en/profil-passwort.png"

  caption="The password section at the bottom of your profile."

The password is not sent to you by email. Make sure to save it right away.

### Sharing passwords

Only share login credentials with the people inside your unit who are responsible for the
website. Never share passwords in WhatsApp or Telegram groups, or in Notion or Google Docs.
A password manager (such as KeePass) is the right place.
